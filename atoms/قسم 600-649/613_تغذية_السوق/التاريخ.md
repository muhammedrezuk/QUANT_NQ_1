## 2.5.0 — 2026-08-27 (found during a full 304-atom audit, not assumed)

**Same family of bug as v2.3.0 above -- another way for a dead feed to
read as HEALTHY.** `self._last_input_at` (the clock `health_check()`'s
`INPUT_STARVED` check reads) was touched at the very TOP of
`_on_source_tick`, before either validation block. A feed sending
nothing but malformed packets (bad bid/ask, missing timestamp, whatever)
refreshed the clock on every single arrival even though every one of
them got dropped -- so `INPUT_STARVED` could never trip as long as
*something*, however garbled, kept arriving on the wire. Combined with
v2.3.0's own finding that `core/api/app.py` strips `details` from every
atom's health response before it reaches the dashboard, this meant the
one channel a human operator actually sees (top-level `state`/`message`)
would keep saying HEALTHY through a 100%-drop-rate feed.

**Fix:** moved `self._last_input_at = time.time()` past both validation
blocks -- it now advances only on a tick that actually parses as usable
(valid bid/ask/timestamp), matching what `INPUT_STARVED` is meant to
measure ("is the feed alive and useful", not "is *anything* arriving on
the wire"). A tick suppressed later for secondary-provider deduplication
still counts as valid input (the feed proved itself alive; the atom just
chose not to forward that one) -- only genuinely malformed packets no
longer count.

New test `test_garbage_only_feed_does_not_fake_freshness`: pins
`_last_input_at` far in the past, feeds five malformed packets, asserts
`health_check()` reports `DEGRADED`/`INPUT_STARVED`. Verified empirically
against a disposable reverted copy of the old ordering first -- it fails
there with `HealthState.HEALTHY` (the exact bug), and passes clean
against every other one of the 6 pre-existing tests, isolating that this
test catches exactly this bug. 7/7 tests, validator 0 violations.
